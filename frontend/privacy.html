Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plank Music handles information when you use the website or Discord bot.

1. Information we receive

Depending on how you use the Service, we may receive:

  • Discord identifiers such as your user, server, channel, and voice-session identifiers, so the bot can join a voice channel and control playback.
  • Search queries, video IDs, queue entries, playback commands, and basic error or diagnostic information.
  • Technical information sent by your browser or network request, such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and request paths, which may appear in hosting or security logs.

We do not ask for your Discord password, and we do not intentionally collect payment-card information through the Service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide search and playback, connect the bot to the correct server and voice channel, maintain queues, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, secure the Service, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing and service providers

We may process information through infrastructure and service providers used to host the website, backend, database, audio resolver, and Discord integration. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or third-party rights. We do not sell personal information.

4. Retention

Queue and player state may be kept while needed to provide playback. Operational logs and security records are retained for a limited period appropriate to troubleshooting and protection. We may delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed, subject to backups and legal requirements.

5. Discord and third-party content

The Discord bot operates within Discord and is subject to Discord’s privacy practices and terms. Search results and media may come from third-party providers with their own policies. Plank Music does not control those providers.

6. Your choices and rights

You can stop using the Service, leave servers where the bot is installed, or contact us to ask about personal information associated with your use. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing. We may need to verify requests before acting.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children who are not permitted to use Discord or the Service under applicable law. If you believe a child provided personal information improperly, contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The revised policy will appear on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@plank.rchqwk.com.
